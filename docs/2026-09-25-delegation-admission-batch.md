# Delegation/admission batch projection court

The marketplace pack projects RDF into
`autofde-lab.delegation-admission-batch/1`. This module is the receiving
transport court.

It deliberately does not pool evidence between cases. Every member is evaluated by
the single-subject delegation court and retains its own subject, boundary,
authority-scope identity, gate, and receipt.

Batch PASS means every member passed. A high-capacity case cannot numerically hide
admission debt in another case. Empty batches are refused to prevent vacuous PASS,
and duplicate exact subject/boundary/authority identities are refused rather than
double-counted.

Aggregate totals are observational metrics only; they carry no cross-subject
authority.

## Product outcome
A first unknown disturbance is lawfully detected/repaired/verified and compiled; a second fresh equivalent disturbance takes the qualified KNOWN path with reduced or zero equivalent exploratory intelligence while preserving all authority and observation boundaries.

## Problem
One successful autonomous repair still repurchases diagnosis and planning the next time. Full autonomics requires the verified repair to compile into MachineExperience and reduce cognition on the equivalent subsequent disturbance.

## Functional requirements
1. Bind first disturbance, repair plan, GALL-003 consequence and GALL-004 verification into one qualified experience subject.
2. Compile MachineExperience only after recovery is independently verified.
3. Start second episode from a fresh runtime/process and equivalent admitted disturbance class.
4. Resolve second episode through MachineExperience when its preconditions match exactly.
5. Require frontier/LLM/planner counters to fall to zero where the compiled reflex fully replaces equivalent work.
6. Still cross ash_a2a authority/receipt boundary for any real consequence; learned experience never grants authority.
7. Independently verify second recovery and compare evidence identity.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Episode 1 begins UNKNOWN and positively executes the repair loop.
2. Verified MachineExperience artifact is emitted.
3. Episode 2 begins fresh with same semantic disturbance class and hits compiled experience.
4. Equivalent frontier/LLM allocation is zero; planner zero where fully compiled out.
5. Episode 2 still receives fresh authority and produces fresh consequence receipt.
6. Independent verification passes both episodes.
7. Changing disturbance preconditions invalidates the reflex and returns to UNKNOWN/candidate planning.

## Evidence product
